# Product Context: Epic SJSU Campus Ministry Web Application

## Overview
Epic is a collegiate campus ministry at San José State University (affiliated with Cru / Epic Movement, Asian American rooted and warmly welcoming all students). This web application serves as the primary digital home and community hub for incoming freshmen, current students, and student leaders.

## Target Audience
1. **Prospective & New Students (Freshmen & Transfers)**: Looking for genuine Christian community, friendships, small groups, and guidance navigating college life at SJSU.
2. **Current Community Members**: Checking upcoming event schedules, weekly meeting locations, retreats, and small group details.
3. **Student Leaders & Ministry Staff**: Managing outreach, accessing the internal portal, and generating print-ready campus fliers and digital social media announcements.

## Modes & Surfaces
- **Mode: Persuade & Welcome (Public Pages)**:
  - Welcome (Home): Compelling editorial hero, student testimonies, mission pillars, clear next steps ("I'm New Here", "Find a Life Group").
  - About Us: Story, values, leadership team, staff bios.
  - Community: Life Groups (small groups by dorms/year), mentoring, campus fellowship.
  - Connect: Contact intake form with ride/mentor matching, social links, campus meeting pins.
  - Events Hub: Weekly rhythms schedule + interactive upcoming calendar.
- **Mode: Operate (Leader Portal & Flyer Studio)**:
  - Top-right Sign In modal (Guest vs. Leader Mode).
  - Unlocked in Events Hub: Canva-style Flyer Creator Studio with live canvas preview, template presets, customizable copy, and 1-click PNG/Print export.

## Core Directives & Hard Constraints
1. **Zero Emojis**: Absolute rule. Do not use emoji characters in code, markup, copy, badges, or icons. Use crisp inline vector SVGs and clean typography.
2. **Aesthetic & Elevation**: SJSU Spartan Navy (`#0B1B3D`), Warm Radiant Gold (`#D4AF37`), Silver accents (`#E2E8F0`, `#94A3B8`), and Warm Alabaster Canvas (`#FAF9F6`).
3. **Typography**: Playfair Display (Serif) for headings; Plus Jakarta Sans (Geometric Sans) for high-legibility UI and body copy.
4. **Performance & Reliability**: Zero external heavy framework overhead; fast vanilla modular architecture.
